Punchboard logs daily work hours on a calendar, tracks vacation and sick days, and estimates your gross and net pay — with tax, deductions, and currency conversion built in. Your data lives in a plain file you choose, not someone else's server.

Everything you need to track a work week

Calendar-based tracking

Log hours per day as a duration or a time range, right from a monthly grid.

Day exceptions

Mark vacation, sick leave, or holidays — excluded automatically from expected hours.

Extra hours

Track overtime separately from your regular schedule.

Salary estimation

Set an hourly rate, tax percentage, and deductions to see estimated gross and net pay.

Currency conversion

Show a second currency alongside your primary one, using a manual conversion rate.

Yearly overview

A chart of hours worked per month, plus weekly totals in the calendar view.

Import / export

Back up or move your data as a plain JSON file, any time.

Your data, your file

No account, no server. Store your data anywhere — iCloud, Dropbox, or a local folder.
